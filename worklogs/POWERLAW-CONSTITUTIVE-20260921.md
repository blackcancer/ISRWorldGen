# Regularized power-law sheet candidate

Base: 96d857b0 on codex/rheology-20260921. No main generator is replaced. Erosion remains suspended.

The previous Newtonian sheet reduced relief. First isolate homogeneous versus heterogeneous resistance with identical initial material, prescribed plate velocities, grids, coupling length and schedule. Then compare a regularized n=3 constitutive law using the SAME material prior and reference length. Subsequent forcing evolves with material; it is not held artificially equal after histories diverge.

The new operator derives stress and tangent from a convex discrete dissipation potential. For each cell Q=N+sum(Hcorner*gamma^2)/(8*mu0), N=ex^2+ez^2+(ex+ez)^2 and derivatives use coupling length L. Hcorner is the fixed harmonic base viscosity of its four cells. mu=mu0*(1+Q/Q0)^(-a), Q0=2*(L*rate0)^2, a=(1-1/n)/2. Corner stresses follow the derivative of the same potential, not an independently re-averaged nonlinear coefficient. At n=1 the exact old harmonic-corner stencil is recovered. Positive curvature follows a<1/2; Newton-CG uses its full tangent and an energy line search. Acceptance recomputes the nonlinear force residual at updated viscosity. No viscosity floor, height filter, sea-level change or tolerance relaxation.

Default reference strain rate .02 per MODEL time and n=3 are explicit uncalibrated priors. This is not brittle damage, inherited fault reactivation, fragmentation, mantle dynamics, slab pull or geographical acceptance. Conceptual sources: England & McKenzie 1982 doi:10.1111/j.1365-246X.1982.tb04969.x with their 1983 correction doi:10.1111/j.1365-246X.1983.tb03328.x; our discrete operator is separately derived, not claimed to reproduce those papers. Newton energy motivation: Rudi, Shih & Stadler 2020 arXiv:2003.11115, not their full viscoplastic implementation.
